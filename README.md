## 🌍 Who We Are

**CS Made Easy** is a student-led educational initiative working to make computer science more accessible to students across Ethiopia.

We believe that talent is everywhere, but access to quality education and practical learning opportunities is not. Through programming education, learning resources, mentorship, and collaborative projects, we help students move beyond theory and develop the skills to build real solutions.

Our mission is simple: **make computer science accessible, practical, and meaningful for every learner.**

---

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Computer Science Education:** Introduce students to programming and core computing concepts through accessible learning materials.
- **Practical Learning:** Encourage hands-on experimentation, independent problem-solving, and project-based learning.
- **AI & Machine Learning:** Collaborate on educational programs that introduce students to artificial intelligence and its applications, including courses taught in Amharic.
- **Mentorship & Community:** Connect learners with opportunities to explore, build, and grow together.

### 📚 CS Made Easy

### ☕ Buna Code

## 🤝 Collaborations

We work with educators, researchers, and fellow initiatives to expand access to computer science education.

Our AI & Machine Learning educational program, delivered in collaboration with **Wongel Dawit**, Researcher at the Language and Spatial Technologies Lab at CMU-Africa, introduces learners to AI concepts, mathematical foundations, and practical applications in Amharic.
